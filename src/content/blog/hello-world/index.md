This site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build), Tailwind CSS, and Markdown/MDX,
themed with [Catppuccin](https://catppuccin.com) (Latte for light mode, Mocha for dark),
and deployed to Cloudflare Workers.

## Why

I wanted a place to write about what I build and learn. Nothing fancier than that.
